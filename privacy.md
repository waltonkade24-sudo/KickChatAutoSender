# Privacy Policy — Kick Chat Auto-Sender

Last updated: February 26, 2026

## Overview
Kick Chat Auto-Sender is a Chrome extension that automates and manages chat messages on Kick.com. We take your privacy seriously.

## Data Collection
This extension does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data stays on your device.

## Data Storage
The extension uses Chrome's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) solely to save:
- Your bookmarked messages
- Your last used message and interval setting

This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to any server.

## Permissions
- **tabs** — to detect the active Kick.com tab
- **storage** — to save your bookmarks and preferences locally
- **scripting / activeTab** — to interact with the Kick.com chat input

## Third Parties
This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking.
